# Export to video

A narrated PPTX generated by PPVoice can be exported to a video (MP4). The narration, subtitles, animations, and slide timing are all included in the video.

## Export from PPVoice (requires PowerPoint)

If PowerPoint is installed, PPVoice can export the video directly.

1. Turn on **Also make a video** at the top
2. Click **Generate**

After generating the narrated PPTX, PPVoice exports `<output name>.mp4` with PowerPoint. Choose the resolution in **Video resolution** on the **Other** tab (default: 1080p). Exporting takes time depending on the length of the slides.

- PowerPoint opens a temporary copy. Presentations you have open in PowerPoint are not affected
- Click **▶ Play the slideshow** after generating to check it as a slide show
- From the command line, run `ppvoice-cli generate input.pptx --video` → [CLI](cli.md)
- You can turn off working with PowerPoint with **Use PowerPoint** on the **Other** tab

## Export with PowerPoint

You can also use PowerPoint's built-in feature.

1. Open the generated PPTX in PowerPoint
2. Choose **File → Export → Create a Video**
3. Choose **Use Recorded Timings and Narrations**
4. Click **Create Video**

!!! tip
    Before exporting, play the slide show once to check the timing of the narration, subtitles, and animations.
